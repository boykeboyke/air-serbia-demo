# Air Serbia — Claims Policy Knowledge Base

## EU Regulation EC 261/2004 — Flight Disruptions

**Scope:** Applies to ALL flights departing from an EU/EEA airport (regardless of carrier), AND flights operated by an EU carrier arriving at an EU/EEA airport. Air Serbia is a non-EU carrier — EC 261/2004 applies ONLY when the flight departs from an EU/EEA airport (e.g. CDG, MAD, BCN, FCO, VIE, LHR etc.). Does NOT apply to BEG-departing flights.

**Compensation (Art. 7) — only when NOT caused by extraordinary circumstances:**
- ≤ 1,500 km → EUR 250
- 1,501–3,500 km (or intra-EU flights > 1,500 km) → EUR 400
- > 3,500 km (non-intra-EU) → EUR 600
- Reduced 50% if passenger is rerouted and arrives less than 2h/3h/4h late (by distance band)

**Cancellation (Art. 5):** Full refund OR rerouting + right to care + compensation (unless >14 days notice, or extraordinary circumstances). Carrier must PROVE extraordinary circumstance.

**Delay — right to care (Art. 9) — applies EVEN in extraordinary circumstances:**
- 2h+ delay for ≤1,500 km: meals/refreshments, 2 free communications
- 3h+ delay for 1,501–3,500 km: same
- 4h+ delay for >3,500 km: same
- Overnight delay: hotel accommodation + transfer to/from airport

**Flight diversion:** Treated as delay at destination. Carrier must provide onward transport to booked destination, or refund + return flight to origin. Care obligations apply.

**Denied boarding (Art. 4):** Voluntary (voucher offer first) then involuntary — same compensation as cancellation + immediate right to care.

**Extraordinary circumstances (no compensation, but care still owed):** Severe weather (thunderstorms, hail, volcanic ash, fog beyond normal), ATC strikes/restrictions, political instability, security threats, hidden aircraft defects not attributable to the carrier. Normal technical issues are NOT extraordinary circumstances.

**Claim deadline:** Varies by country; passengers typically have 2–6 years. Claims submitted to the operating carrier.

## Montreal Convention (1999) — Baggage and Delay

**Scope:** Applies to ALL international Air Serbia flights.

### Damaged Baggage (Art. 17.2)
- PIR (Property Irregularity Report) must be filed at the arrival airport immediately upon noticing damage, or within 7 days of receipt
- Written complaint to airline must follow within 7 days of receipt
- Liability limit: approx. EUR 1,550 per passenger (legal unit: 1,288 SDR under Montreal Convention)
- Carrier may apply depreciation based on bag age and condition at time of damage
- Required documents: PIR reference, boarding pass, baggage tag, damage photos, bag purchase receipt, repair/replacement quote

### Delayed Baggage (Art. 19)
- Written complaint within 21 days of delivery date
- Interim essential expenses (toiletries, clothing) claimable with receipts
- Liability limit: approx. EUR 1,550 per passenger

### Lost Baggage
- Declared lost after 21 days from the date the carrier confirmed the delay
- Liability limit: approx. EUR 1,550 per passenger
- Passenger must provide list of contents with estimated values

## Air Serbia — Official Passenger Rights Policy (from airserbia.com)

### Uskraćeno ukrcavanje (Denied Boarding — Overbooking)

Uskraćeno ukrcavanje znači da putniku nije dozvoljeno ukrcavanje uprkos propisnoj prijavi i potvrđenoj rezervaciji u roku za ček-in, osim zbog opravdanih razloga (zdravlje, bezbednost, nedostatak putnih isprava).

**Procedure:**
- Air Serbia prvo traži dobrovoljce (uz dogovorene benefite i pravo na odabir između nadoknade ili preusmerenja)
- Ako nema dobrovoljaca, ukrcavanje se uskraćuje prisilno
- Kod prisilnog uskraćivanja Air Serbia isplaćuje:
  - €250 za letove do 1.500 km
  - €400 za letove od 1.500 do 3.500 km
  - €600 za letove preko 3.500 km
- Naknada se smanjuje za 50% ako se ponudi preusmerenje i putnik stigne na odredište sa zakašnjenjem manjim od 2h/3h/4h (zavisno od daljine)
- Na aerodromu BEG isplata po izboru: elektronski bankarski transfer, debitna/kreditna kartica, keš ili EMD vaučer
- Na aerodromima u inostranstvu: pošalji zahtev Podršci putnicima — isplata EMD vaučerom ili bankarskim transferom
- Putnik bira između: (a) pune nadoknade cene karte u roku od 7 dana + povratni let do polazišta; ili (b) preusmerenja pod sličnim uslovima u najkraćem roku ili kasnijeg datuma po izboru putnika
- Besplatne prednosti: obroci/osveženja, 2 besplatna poziva/faks/mejl, hotelski smeštaj ako je potrebno + prevoz do/od aerodroma

### Kašnjenje leta (Flight Delay)

Pravo na besplatne prednosti nastaje kada se očekuje kašnjenje:
- 2h+ za letove do 1.500 km
- 3h+ za letove od 1.500 do 3.500 km
- 4h+ za letove preko 3.500 km

**Besplatne prednosti:** obroci/osveženja + 2 besplatna poziva/faks/mejl

Ako se očekivano vreme polaska pomeri za ceo dan: besplatan hotel + prevoz do/od aerodroma.

Ako je let odložen najmanje 5 sati: putnik ima pravo na punu nadoknadu cene karte u roku od 7 dana za nerealizovane delove putovanja (i realizovane ako let više ne služi svrsi), plus povratni let do polazišta ako je relevantno.

**Napomena:** Kašnjenje ne daje pravo na EC 261 kompenzaciju (€250/€400/€600) — to pravo postoji samo kod uskraćenog ukrcavanja i ukidanja leta, ili kod kašnjenja od 3h+ na dolazištu sudskom praksom (Sturgeon).

### Ukidanje leta (Cancellation)

Air Serbia pruža:
- Izbor između nadoknade (puna cena karte u roku od 7 dana) ili preusmerenja (u najkraćem roku ili po izboru)
- Besplatne prednosti: obroci, 2 poziva/faks/mejl, hotel + prevoz (ako preusmerenje kreće sledećeg dana)
- Kompenzacija: €250 / €400 / €600 po daljini (iste tablice kao kod uskraćenog ukrcavanja)
- Kompenzacija se smanjuje 50% ako alternativni let stiže unutar 2h/3h/4h od originalnog dolaska
- Isplata: EMD vaučer ili bankarski transfer — NE na licu mesta; zahtev se šalje Podršci putnicima

**Kompenzacija se NE isplaćuje ako:**
- Putnik obavešten ≥14 dana unapred
- Obavešten 7–14 dana unapred i ponuđeno preusmerenje (polazak ≤2h ranije, dolazak ≤4h posle originalnog)
- Obavešten <7 dana unapred i ponuđeno preusmerenje (polazak ≤1h ranije, dolazak ≤2h posle originalnog)
- Air Serbia dokaže izuzetne okolnosti koje se nisu mogle izbeći ni razumnim merama

### Premeštanje u nižu klasu (Downgrade)

Putnik ima pravo na povraćaj dela cene karte u roku od 7 dana, u procentu koji zavisi od daljine leta.

### Uslovi primene prava putnika

Prava se primenjuju ako:
- Let polazi sa aerodroma u ECCA zemlji ili stiže na ECCA aerodrom a Air Serbia je operativni prevoznik
- Putnik ima potvrđenu rezervaciju
- Putnik se pojavio na ček-inu u propisanom roku (ili najmanje 45 min pre polaska ako vreme nije navedeno)
- Karta je izdata u okviru programa lojalnosti ili komercijalnog programa Air Serbie ili organizatora

Prava se NE primenjuju na besplatne karte ili snižene cene koje nisu dostupne javnosti.

## Air Serbia — Claims Procedures

### Damaged/Delayed/Lost Baggage
- File PIR at the airport (ground handler: Groundforce at BCN, Swissport at MAD, JAT Ground Services at BEG)
- PIR reference format: [AIRPORT][JU][NUMBER]/[DDMMMYY]/[HHMMtimezone] — e.g. MADJU11257/22JUN26/0743GMT or BCNJU11257/18JUN26/0743GMT
- Submit formal claim to: **guest.support@airserbia.com**
- Required attachments: PIR copy, boarding pass, baggage tag, photos of damage, bag purchase receipt, repair/replacement quote
- Response time target: 20 business days
- Reference format for email subject: [FLIGHT]/[DATE]/BAGGAGE — e.g. JU573/22JUN26/BAGGAGE

### Flight Disruption Claims (EC 261/2004)
- Submit to: **guest.support@airserbia.com**
- Required: PNR/booking reference, flight number and date, route, written description, boarding pass, expense receipts (if claiming care costs)
- If carrier claims extraordinary circumstances, burden of proof lies with the carrier

### Useful Contacts
- Guest Support: guest.support@airserbia.com
- Call centre: +381 11 311 2 123
- Lost & Found BEG: +381 11 209 4 373

## Key Terms (Serbian)
- let (flight) — NEVER "letilica"
- prtljag / kofer (baggage / suitcase)
- aerodrom (airport) — NEVER "vazdušna luka" (that's Croatian)
- naknada / odšteta (compensation) — not "kompenzacija"
- reklamacija / pritužba (claim / complaint)
- zahtev (request) — NEVER "zahtjev" (Croatian)
- izveštaj (report) — NEVER "izvještaj" (Croatian)
- podnet / podneta (filed/submitted) — NEVER "podnešen" (Croatian)
- preusmerjavanje (diversion) — not "preusmjeravanje" (Croatian)
- zbrinjavanje (care/welfare) — not "briga" for this specific regulatory term
- odgovor mejlom (email reply) — NEVER "odgovorni email"
- karta za ukrcavanje (boarding pass) — not "boarding pass" in full Serbian text
- nalepnica prtljaga (baggage tag) — not "baggage tag" in full Serbian text
