How this was built

Exactly what's real, and what we modelled.

A demo should be honest about its seams. Here are ours. Everything we do not flag here is genuinely working.

Real today
  • The agent and its reasoning. A live PolyAI voice agent, not a script.
  • Bilingual switching. Serbian and English, switched at runtime when it hears the caller.
  • The flight-status lookup. A real external flight API call, the same one on this page.
  • Personalization. It greets by name and reads the booking back from the record on call-open.
  • The Studio build. Assembled in PolyAI Studio, the same builder your team would use.
Modelled for this demo
  • The passenger record. One illustrative Elevate profile (Milan Petrović, PNR JU8K2P) stands in for your CRM.
  • Booking, baggage and check-in writes. Confirmed in the demo, not written to a live reservation system.
  • The API shape. A faithful mock of Air Serbia's booking and baggage data, not a link to production.
  • Brand assets. Colours and the wordmark are matched from public Air Serbia assets; type is a close free match.
